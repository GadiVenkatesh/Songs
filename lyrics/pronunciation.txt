# Pronunciation guide (read aloud before recording)

## Place
Telugu: పెరిపి గ్రామం
Roman: Pe-ri-pi GRAA-mam
Say it as two clear words: “Peripi Graamam”. Do not say “Vilg Peripi”.

## MPTC candidate
Telugu: కొరాడ కోటేశ్వరరావు
Roman: Ko-raa-da Ko-tesh-wa-ra-raa-vu
Break for a singer: “Korada | Koteswara Rao”.
Keep the long vowel in “రా” of Korada. Do not merge the full name into one rushed phrase.

## ZPTC candidate
Telugu: వలిరెడ్డి శ్రీనివాస్ నాయుడు
Roman: Va-li-red-di Shree-ni-vaas Naa-yu-du
Break for a singer: “Valireddy | Srinivas | Naidu”.
Keep “శ్రీ” clear; do not swallow the “రెడ్డి” or shorten “నాయుడు”.

## Singing direction
- Tempo: 100–108 BPM, 4/4 folk groove.
- Use a half-beat pause before each candidate's full name.
- Pronounce names conversationally, not with exaggerated melisma.
- In call-and-response lines, leave a small gap between question and answer.
- Ask a native Telugu speaker from the local area to confirm the exact pronunciation of “Valireddy” and “Koteswara Rao” before final export.

## Transliteration used in prompts
Peripi Graamam
Korada Koteswara Rao
Valireddy Srinivas Naidu
